The problem

Real gap jo yeh app solve karti hai

Chhote shehron aur dor-daraz colleges ke students ke paas aksar affordable tuition ya 24/7 academic help ka access nahi hota. Books hain, lecture notes hain — lekin jab raat ko 11 baje koi concept samajh na aaye, poochne wala koi nahi hota.

The fix

Har waqt available, bilingual madad

PadhaiPal ek AI companion hai jo kabhi thakta nahi, dobara-dobara ek hi sawal se irritate nahi hota, aur student ki apni zaban (Roman Urdu/English mix) mein baat karta hai — bilkul waise jaise ek senior classmate samjhata ho.
