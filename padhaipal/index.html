The problem

The real gap this app solves

Students at small-city and rural colleges often can't access affordable tuition or 24/7 academic help. There are textbooks and lecture notes — but when a concept doesn't click at 11pm the night before an exam, there's no one to ask.

The fix

Always-on, bilingual help

PadhaiPal is an AI companion that never gets tired, never gets annoyed by the same question twice, and speaks in whichever language a student is comfortable with — just like a senior classmate explaining things.
